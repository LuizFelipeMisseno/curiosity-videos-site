Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Curiosity Videos is a locally-run content tool. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect

  • TikTok account authorization. When you connect a TikTok account, the tool stores an OAuth access token, refresh token, and your TikTok open ID on your own device. These credentials are used only to upload videos you request.
  • Content you create. Scripts, audio, and video files generated by the tool are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded anywhere except to TikTok when you choose to post.
  • Third-party AI services. Video generation sends script text to OpenAI and text-to-speech requests to ElevenLabs, governed by their respective privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, or share personal data on any server operated by us.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any data.
  • We do not access your TikTok data beyond the permissions you grant during authorization.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens and content are stored only on your device. Delete the token file to revoke stored credentials, or disconnect the app in your TikTok settings to revoke access server-side.
