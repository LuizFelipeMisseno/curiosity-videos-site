Curiosity Videos

Curiosity Videos is a personal content tool that automatically produces short educational videos about mathematics and astronomy, and publishes them to TikTok. It combines AI script writing, text-to-speech narration, and vector motion graphics.

The tool runs locally on its owner's machine. It posts only to the TikTok account authorized by its owner.
